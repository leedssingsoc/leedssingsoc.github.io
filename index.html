New to Leeds?

Questions freshers ask us.

Paiseh to ask? Message us on Instagram. We don't bite.

Do I have to be Singaporean to join?

No. Anyone at the University of Leeds can join. Plenty of members just love Singaporean food and company.

How do I become a member?

Buy a membership on our Leeds University Union page. Members get discounts and perks at our sponsors.

What's the time difference with home?

Singapore is 8 hours ahead of the UK in winter (GMT) and 7 hours ahead in summer (BST, late March to late October). Right now it's 8 hours ahead. The clock at the top of this page does the maths for you.

Which phone plan and bank should I get?

Most students pick up a SIM-only plan from a UK network in their first week, and many open an account with an app-based bank because it's quick to set up. Ask us at any event and we'll tell you what members use.

Should I register with a doctor?

Yes. Register with a GP near where you live as soon as you arrive, so you're covered before you need it. The university's student support pages explain how.

Where do I find Singaporean food in Leeds?

There's no Singaporean restaurant in Leeds yet, so Malaysian food is the closest taste of home. Try Zan Asian on The Headrow. The city centre also has plenty of Asian food, and these sponsors give members a discount: Regency Palace (Cantonese), Viet Tasty (Vietnamese), Zaap Thai (Thai), Blue Sakura and Maki & Ramen (Japanese), and Memento (tea and noodles). Asian supermarkets in the city centre stock most of the snacks and sauces you'll miss.
